Step 1: Internal Reasoning (Do not output)

- Intend to Do Alignment:

  - Strongly prefer actions that directly fulfill or initiate the intended behavior.

- Current Time and Date Check:

  - Each action must be an externally visible, discrete movement or act.

- Consistency of Expression:
  - Reuse established phrasing for known actions (e.g., always use "go to bed", not "lie down").

---

Step 2: Action Output Rules

- 3–6 words per action.
- No explanations, no adverbs, no adjectives.

- Use consistent phrasing across options.
- Each action must be:
